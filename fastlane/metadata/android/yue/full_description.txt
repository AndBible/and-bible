<b>功能強大嘅研經工具</b>

"AndBible: 研經工具" is a powerful, easy-to-use, offline Bible study app for Android — a versatile toolkit of high-quality features serving many study styles, from casual reading to in-depth research. More than a reader, it's a tool for serious personal study.

Developed by Bible readers, for Bible readers — a non-profit, open-source community project that is completely free and contains no ads.

熱門嘅聖經譯本如和合本、新譯本、KJV、NASB，以及常見嘅註解如Matthew Henry及John Gill等。文件之多，未能盡錄。

<b>強大嘅研經功能</b>

此程式具有原創、有深度嘅功能，幫助讀者有一個流暢嘅研經體驗。以下係一啲重點功能：

 * 分割視窗可以幫助比較譯本同參考註解
 * 工作區可以自定研經嘅環境
 * Strong's 參考方便查找希臘文及希伯來文分析
 * 撳任何連結就可以跳去交叉參考、註腳同註解（Gill、Matthew Henry之類），或者交叉參考集（Treasure of Scripture Knowledge, TSKe）。
 * Optional AI study agent (opt-in only — requires your own API key for an external service): explore your installed commentaries and dictionaries with AI assistance in your chosen language, plus other creative study workflows. A built-in permission system keeps you in full control of what the agent can do.
 * Flexible full-text search across one or many Bible translations simultaneously
 * 包括個人筆記的書籤及醒目提示
 * 研習筆記可以讓你喺聽講道時，新增筆記及參考。
 * Reading and memorization tracking: set reading goals, track progress, and memorize verses with interactive Word Scramble, Word Order, Word Blur, and Type It modes
 * 進階嘅文字轉語音功能及語音書籤，以提供一個流暢嘅聆聽聖經體驗。
 * Cross-device sync: keep your workspaces, bookmarks, notes, and reading progress in sync across all your devices
 * 龐大嘅文件庫：700種語言、超過1500份聖經譯本、神學註釋、字典、地點及基督教書籍。全部都係由Crosswire及其他SWORD資料庫授權分發。
 * 原生支援MyBible、MySword及EPUB檔案，讓你可以豐富你嘅收藏。
