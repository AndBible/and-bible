#### Background

[Tuomas Airaksinen Consulting](https://tuomasairaksinen.fi/consulting/), company of the lead developer, is providing a way to sponsor AndBible development. You can support AndBible development financially via [the webshop](https://shop.andbible.org).

I am reporting in monthly basis the significant numbers in this [google sheets document](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1BDH1nq9X23s6DpPVrTN8KGbFgl22weeRDAX5O3zYHB4/edit#gid=529567944).

See also: [financial report for Q4/2023](/2024/01/04/financial-report-for-q4-2024/).

#### Q1 / 2024

During this period, the pricing for sponsored work was 60€ / hour + VAT (VAT is applicable only for EU customers). In the table below, I am summarising Q1 hours and as a comparison also earlier months. What I consider eligible as payable development hours is [discussed in this blog post](/2023/12/16/what-do-i-consider-as-paid-sponsored-development/).

| Date | Sponsored hours | Development hours | **Development** **hours** (not sponsored) | Paid in % |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 9/2023 | 2.68 | 21.05 | 18.37 | 12.73% |
| 10/2023 | 4.92 | 32.65 | 25.06 | 23.25% |
| 11/2023 | 14.97 | 37.5 | 22.53 | 39.92% |
| 12/2023 | 15.11 | 13.98 | 0 | 100.00% |
| **1/2024** | 3.77 | 12.67 | 4.9 | 38.67% |
| **2/2024** | 1 | 3.65 | 1 | 27.40% |
| **3/2024** | 1.8 | 2.4 | 1.8 | 75.00% |

Observations:

- 👎 Total support has dried down quite a lot. There are some faithful regular sponsors though.
- 👍AndBible is still maintained and support emails are responded (sometimes a bit late, but responded anyway), and the app is functioning well for most users, receiving many 5 star ratings every month.
- In this period I was not able to do so much work because I had holiday 11th Feb-9th March away from home
- If the amount of support increases by time, I will be able to spend significantly more hours on developing AndBible

#### Breakdown of tasks (December 2023-March 2024)

In December, I started timing different tasks so that it is possible to analyze how the hours have been used. Here is the breakdown how paid hours were used:

| Paid tasks | Dec 2023 | Q1/2024 | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Support | 3.82 | 1.48 | 5.3 |
| Maintenance | 8.03 | 0.92 | 8.95 |
| Feature | 2.13 | 0 | 2.13 |
| Total | 13.98 | 2.4 | 16.38 |

In other words, so far statistics look like this:

- Support (help.andbible@gmail.com email mainly): 5.3 hours = 32.4%
- Maintenance tasks (bug fixing etc): 8.95 hours = 54.6 %
- New feature development: 2.13 hours = 13 %
