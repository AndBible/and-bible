#### Background

[Tuomas Airaksinen Consulting](https://tuomasairaksinen.fi/consulting/), company of the lead developer, is providing a way to sponsor AndBible development. You can support AndBible development financially via [the webshop](https://shop.andbible.org).

I am reporting in monthly basis the significant numbers in this [google sheets document](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1BDH1nq9X23s6DpPVrTN8KGbFgl22weeRDAX5O3zYHB4/edit#gid=529567944).

**Note**: During my christmas holiday I also implemented [better E-Ink support](https://github.com/AndBible/and-bible/pull/3354) in my own time (i.e. work was self-sponsored). I did not calculate those hours, but it was roughly 3 full days total. This work is not included in any of these numbers.

Observations:

- 👎Total support is still far from **goal (4 full working days per month)** but there has been some improvement.
- 👍Nevertheless, I have been able to maintain AndBible by fixing bugs and responding to user emails.
- 👍 E-ink support is great!
- 👍 App is still great, even with less development :-)
- If the amount of support increases by time, I will be able to spend significantly more hours on developing AndBible.

#### Breakdown of tasks (December 2023-December 2024)

In December 2023, I started timing different tasks so that it is possible to analyze how the hours have been used. Here is the breakdown how paid hours were used:

| Paid tasks | Dec 2023 | Q1/2024 | Q2/2024 | Q3/2024 | Q4/2024 | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Support | 3.82 | 1.48 | 5.42 | 7.28 | 7.35 | 18.59 |
| Maintenance | 8.03 | 0.92 | 14.57 | 6.25 | 8.03 | 29.77 |
| Feature | 2.13 | 0 | 15.05 | 0.00 | 0.09 | 17.18 |
| Reviewing contributor's code | 0 | 0 | 0 | 2.28 | 4.02 | 2.28 |
| Total | 13.98 | 2.4 | 35.04 | 16.40 | 19.40 | 67.28 |

In other words, in Q4 statistics look like this:

- Support (help.andbible@gmail.com email mainly): 7.35 hours = 37.9%
- Maintenance tasks (bug fixing etc): 8.03 hours = 41.4 %
- New feature development: 0 hours = 0 %
- Reviewing contributor's code: 4.02 hours = 20.7%
