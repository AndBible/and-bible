#### Background

[Tuomas Airaksinen Consulting](https://tuomasairaksinen.fi/consulting/), company of the lead developer, is providing a way to sponsor AndBible development. You can support AndBible development financially via [the webshop](https://shop.andbible.org).

I am reporting in monthly basis the significant numbers in this [google sheets document](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1BDH1nq9X23s6DpPVrTN8KGbFgl22weeRDAX5O3zYHB4/edit#gid=529567944). Now it is time to write first full quarterly report and analyze the results briefly.

#### Q4 / 2023

During this period, the pricing for sponsored work was 60€ / hour + VAT (VAT is applicable only for EU customers). In the table below, I am summarising Q4 hours and also september (when I started). What I consider eligible as payable development hours is [discussed in this blog post](/2023/12/16/what-do-i-consider-as-paid-sponsored-development/).

| Date | Sponsored hours | Development hours | **Development** **hours** (not sponsored) | Paid in % |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 9/2023 | 2.68 | 21.05 | 18.37 | 12.73% |
| 10/2023 | 4.92 | 32.65 | 25.06 | 23.25% |
| 11/2023 | 14.97 | 37.5 | 22.53 | 39.92% |
| 12/2023 | 15.11 | 13.98 | 0 | 100.00% |

Same in graphic form:

![](/media/blog/2024/01/image.webp)

Observations:

- 👍 Amount of sponsored hours has increased every month. This is encouraging!
- 👎 Still way to go to goal of 4 fully paid working days (30 hours) per month
- In December I was working less on AndBible. 1.13 paid hours was left to be used in January 2024.

#### Breakdown of tasks (December 2023)

In December, I started timing different tasks so that it is possible to analyze how the hours have been used. Here is the breakdown how paid hours were used:

- Support (help.andbible@gmail.com email mainly): 3.82 hours
- Maintenance tasks (bug fixing etc): 8.03 hours
- New feature development: 2.13 hours

Same in graphic form:

![](/media/blog/2024/01/image-1.webp)

As it can be seen, email support is taking rather a lot of time. That work I have tried to get to volunteers, but still there's quite a lot of work left for me. I have also tried to make managing this more efficient by having AndBible make as easy-to-process bug reports as possible.

In addition to above mentioned paid work, I was doing [unpaid work](/2023/12/16/what-do-i-consider-as-paid-sponsored-development/) as follows:

- Youtube videos (Tips & Tricks), advertising, blog: 10.34 hours.

#### Closing words

I want to thank every AndBible sponsor for making it possible to keep the app well maintained, supported and developing. Wishing you all also blessed new year 2024!
