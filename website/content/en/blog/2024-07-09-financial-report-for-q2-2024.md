#### No more VAT (=value added tax)!

I learned in June 2024 that as long as sales in EU are below 10 000 € / year I am not required to collect VAT. So from now on sponsorship is VAT-free. In 2025, this limit will increase to 20 000 € as far as I have understood, but will confirm that later (if needed).

Earlier, VAT has not been collected for non-EU sales, but for EU sales VAT based on customer country has been collected (I have sent receipts manually).
