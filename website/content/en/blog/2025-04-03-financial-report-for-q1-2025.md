#### Background

[Tuomas Airaksinen Consulting](https://tuomasairaksinen.fi/consulting/), company of the lead developer, is providing a way to sponsor AndBible development. You can support AndBible development financially via [the webshop](https://shop.andbible.org).

I am reporting in monthly basis the significant numbers in this [google sheets document](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1BDH1nq9X23s6DpPVrTN8KGbFgl22weeRDAX5O3zYHB4/edit#gid=529567944).

**Note**: We were moving with my wife in March so in Feb-March period so most of my excess time was going to related tasks. I was receiving more funding than I was able to work for. So after March, I have 11.34 hours of sponsorship left, not yet used. This will be used in April, Lord willing.

Observations:

- 👍Support has been increased significantly in last 3 months. I feel blessed about this development, and very happy to see that there are other people sharing the (financial) burden of AndBible development with me!
- 👍New cool features that sponsorship has enabled me to do: [NextCloud support for device sync](/2025/02/22/using-andbible-device-synchronization-with-nextcloud/) and custom icons for bookmarks.
- 👎Total support has still room for improvement (goal is 4 full working days per month)

#### Breakdown of tasks (2024-2025)

Here is the breakdown how paid hours were used:

| Paid tasks | Q1/2024 | Q2/2024 | Q3/2024 | Q4/2024 | Q1/2025 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Support | 1.48 | 5.42 | 7.28 | 7.35 | 3.0 |
| Maintenance | 0.92 | 14.57 | 6.25 | 8.03 | 6.9 |
| Feature | 0 | 15.05 | 0.00 | 0.09 | 30.3 |
| Reviewing contributor's code | 0 | 0 | 2.28 | 4.02 | 0 |
| Total | 2.4 | 35.04 | 16.40 | 19.40 | 40.2 |

In other words, in Q1 statistics look like this:

- Support (help.andbible@gmail.com email mainly): 3.0 hours = 7.5%
- Maintenance tasks (bug fixing etc): 6.9 hours = 17.2 %
- New feature development: 30.3 hours = 75.4 %
- Reviewing contributor's code: 0 hours = 0 %

Some analysis/remarks:

- Due to special period (moving etc.) I was not having a lot of time for almost anything. Still because of some significant sponsorships, I wanted to spend some time in feature development. During this period I was clearly neglecting support (in fact, people are still waiting for replies when I'm writing this).
- I believe that things from my point of view are normalizing in Q2/2025. Though feature development is having priority as I think that's what sponsors want me to do most.
