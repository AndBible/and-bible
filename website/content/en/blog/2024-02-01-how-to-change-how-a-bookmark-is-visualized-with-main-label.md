In AndBible, bookmark labels are used for

- tagging / categorizing bookmarks into different categories
- to mark that a bookmark belongs to a specific studypad
- to adjust how a bookmark looks like

As bookmark can have many labels, only one label determines the visualization of the bookmark. That is the function of Main Label.

In this video I'll show how you can adjust how bookmarks are visualized in bibles / documents by changing the Main Label. You are also advised to see separate [in-depth tutorial into Bookmarks, Notes, Labels and StudyPad](https://youtu.be/w6IqjORyG9E).
