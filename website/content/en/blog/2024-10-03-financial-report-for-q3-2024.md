#### Background

Observations:

- 👎Support is drying again. I wonder why - but I am guessing one reason could be that I have been quite non-active in Youtube and other social media.
- 👍Nevertheless, I have been able to maintain AndBible by fixing bugs and responding to user emails.
- 👍 App is still great, even with less development :-)
- If the amount of support increases by time, I will be able to spend significantly more hours on developing AndBible.

#### Breakdown of tasks (December 2023-September 2024)

In December 2023, I started timing different tasks so that it is possible to analyze how the hours have been used. Here is the breakdown how paid hours were used:

| Paid tasks | Dec 2023 | Q1/2024 | Q2/2024 | Q3/2024 | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Support | 3.82 | 1.48 | 5.42 | 7.28 | 18.59 |
| Maintenance | 8.03 | 0.92 | 14.57 | 6.25 | 29.77 |
| Feature | 2.13 | 0 | 15.05 | 0.00 | 17.18 |
| Reviewing contributor's code | 0 | 0 | 0 | 2.28 | 2.28 |
| Total | 13.98 | 2.4 | 35.04 | 16.40 | 67.28 |

In other words, in Q4 statistics look like this:

- Support (help.andbible@gmail.com email mainly): 7.28 hours = 48%
- Maintenance tasks (bug fixing etc): 29.77 hours = 38.1 %
- New feature development: 0 hours = 0 %
- Reviewing contributor's code: 2.28 hours = 13.9%
