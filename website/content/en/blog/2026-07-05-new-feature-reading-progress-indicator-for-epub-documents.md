Ever found yourself deep in a lengthy EPUB commentary or Bible book, wondering how much further you have to go? AndBible's new Reading Progress indicator answers exactly that — at a glance, and without getting in your way.

https://www.youtube.com/shorts/BHC2A7AwYBI

## What it does

When enabled, a small, unobtrusive text overlay appears in the corner of the reading view and updates live as you scroll. It tells you how far you’ve read through what you’re currently reading:

- **EPUB books:** progress through the *whole book*, shown as a percentage plus an estimated page — for example `47% · 142/300`.
- **Bibles and commentaries:** progress through the *current book*, shown as a percentage plus your chapter — for example `47% · 23/50`.

The indicator is smart about context. The meaning of “how far through” adapts to what you’re reading, so a single setting does the right thing everywhere.
