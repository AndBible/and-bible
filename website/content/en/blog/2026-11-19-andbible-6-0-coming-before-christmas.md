AndBible 6.0 is getting close. **Our goal is to release it before Christmas**, so that everyone
can use and try out AndBible 6.0 on **Christmas Eve at the latest**. That is a goal, not a
guarantee: if something important is not ready, we will take the time it needs. But that is
what we are working towards, and we wanted you to know.

Here is a short teaser:

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TEASER_YOUTUBE_ID

## What 6.0 brings

AndBible 6.0 takes AndBible beyond Android. It is one app for your phone, your tablet and your
computer:

- **Every platform.** Android phones and tablets, iPhone, iPad and Mac, Windows and Linux. On a
  Mac it is the iPad app, on any Mac with Apple Silicon.
- **The same Bible study everywhere.** Parallel Bibles with Strong's numbers, cross-references,
  commentaries and maps, bookmarks, notes and StudyPads, in a renewed interface that every
  platform shares.
- **Sync between your devices.** Google Drive, Nextcloud and WebDAV work on every platform, and
  iCloud Drive on iPhone, iPad and Mac. Add a bookmark with a note on your phone, and the next
  sync brings it to your computer. See [Cloud synchronization](/docs/cloud_sync/).
- **An AI Bible study assistant**, if you want one. Choose a passage and a prompt, and it
  explains, summarizes or translates, working from the commentaries and dictionaries you have
  installed. It is optional: you use your own API key from the provider you choose, and like any
  AI it can make mistakes, so check its answers. See [AI Bible study assistant](/docs/ai/).

The full list of changes is on the [Release 6.0](/docs/releases/release_6_0/) page.

## How to get it when it lands

- **Android:** Google Play or F-Droid, as before.
- **iPhone, iPad and Mac:** the App Store.
- **Windows:** the Microsoft Store, or the signed MSI installer from our
  [GitHub releases page](https://github.com/AndBible/and-bible/releases).
- **Linux:** a `.deb` package or an AppImage from the
  [GitHub releases page](https://github.com/AndBible/and-bible/releases).

[Getting started](/docs/getting_started/) walks you through the installation on each platform.

Can't wait? The public beta is open now: see
[how to join the AndBible 6.0 public beta](/2026/11/07/andbible-6-0-public-beta/), and please tell
us what you find.
