In this video I'll show how you can set up custom document repositories in AndBible. There are not many custom repositories available at the moment, but [users can create those](https://github.com/AndBible/and-bible/wiki/Custom-repositories) and share them via their own channels as well as our [Matrix/Telegram group](https://github.com/AndBible/and-bible/wiki/Support) (as long as they are legal).

AndBible supports MyBible custom repositories and Sword repositories.
