## Printing Tips

- **Home printing:** Use the “Fit to page” option in your printer settings for best results.
- **Professional printing:** Send the professional version to your local print shop for high-quality output on glossy or matte paper.
- **Suggested paper:** A4 or Letter size, standard or glossy paper.

## Usage

These materials are free to use for promoting AndBible. Feel free to print and distribute them at churches, Bible study groups, conferences, or community events.
