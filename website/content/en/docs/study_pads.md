# Study Pads

A Study Pad collects everything you have on one topic: Bible references and your
own notes, in an order you choose. Every [label](labels.md) has its own Study Pad.
A label can be anything: a topic such as ‘Holiness’ or ‘Salvation’, or an event
such as ‘Sermon on 24 February’. Study Pads are well suited to sermon notes and
personal Bible study.

## Opening a Study Pad {#opening-a-study-pad}

- From the main menu (☰), click *Study Pads*. This lists your labels; click one
    to open its Study Pad. You can search the list by
    label name or by the content of the Study Pads.
- From a bookmark: in the verse action dialog, click the Study Pad button on the
    bookmark card to open the Study Pad of one of its labels. See
    [the bookmark card](bookmarks.md#the-bookmark-card).
- From the [Label settings](labels.md#open-label-settings), you can also manage
    the labels themselves.

A new Study Pad is empty. Its title is the name of the label.

## Adding entries

A Study Pad has two kinds of entries.

**Bible references.** [Add a bookmark](bookmarks.md#creating-a-bookmark) in a Bible
window and give it the Study Pad's label. It appears in the Study Pad at once,
and so does every later bookmark with that label, including bookmarks on just a
few words. A [note](notes.md) added to such a bookmark shows under its verse.

To save effort, set the label to be ‘auto-assigned’ in
[Label settings](labels.md#change-label-settings): every new bookmark then gets
the label automatically and shows up in the Study Pad.

**Text entries.** For a general note that does not belong to a verse, click the
plus (+) button in the Study Pad and type the text. Changes are saved
automatically. Click a text entry to edit it; if you prefer to edit only with the
edit button, enable *Disable Study Pad click-to-edit* under Application
preferences > Look & feel.

## The entry toolbar

Each entry has a toolbar with these buttons:

- **Plus (+):** add a new text entry after this one.
- **Edit (pencil):** edit the entry's text or note.
- **Outdent / Indent:** change the indentation level of the entry.
- **Collapse / Expand:** for a Bible reference, hide or show its text and note,
    so it takes less space.
- **Info:** for a Bible reference, open the bookmark's details.
- **Trash can:** delete the entry.

## Moving entries

Drag an entry by its drag handle to move it. A Bible reference moves together
with its note.

## Deleting entries

- A text entry asks for confirmation and is then deleted.
- A Bible reference whose bookmark has only this label asks for confirmation and
    deletes the bookmark.
- If the bookmark has other labels too, you choose: *Only label* removes the
    bookmark from this Study Pad but keeps it with its other labels;
    *Whole bookmark* deletes the bookmark itself.

## Exporting and importing Study Pads

- To share one open Study Pad (as an HTML page, as a Study Pad file, or its
    bookmarks as CSV), use the window menu. See [Sharing a Study Pad](share.md#sharing-a-study-pad).
- To export several Study Pads at once, open *Study Pads* from the main menu or
    [Label settings](labels.md#open-label-settings), then in the ⋮ menu choose
    *Export Study Pads*. Select the Study Pads to include and choose where to
    save or share the file.
- To import Study Pads that someone has exported, choose *Import Study Pads*
    in the same ⋮ menu and select the file.

## Summary

Every label has its own Study Pad. When you add a label to a verse, the verse
appears in the Study Pad automatically, and so do the notes added to the
bookmark. You can add your own text entries between them and arrange everything
by dragging.
