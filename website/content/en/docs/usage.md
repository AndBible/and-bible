# Study workflows {#usage}

## Overview

Below are suggestions on how you might like to configure the app for different use scenarios.
*AndBible* is highly flexible — you can set up multiple [Workspaces](workspaces.md) for different study contexts
and switch between them easily.

## Bible Study

Open a Bible in one window and a commentary in another using [Windows](windows.md).
Windows are synchronised by default: put them in the same group with the `Synchronise` item
of the window menu (`Group 1` to `Group 6`, or `Disable synchronize`) so the commentary
follows your Bible reading. See [Synchronising windows](windows.md#synchronising-windows).
Use [Cross-References](documents.md#cross-references) to discover related passages and
add [Bookmarks](bookmarks.md) with [Notes](notes.md) as you study.

## Topical Study

Create a dedicated [workspace](workspaces.md) for your topic. Use [Study Pads](study_pads.md)
to collect verses and notes on a specific theme. The quickest way is to create a label for the
topic and switch on *Auto-assign label to new bookmarks* for it in
[Label settings](labels.md#change-label-settings): every new bookmark in that workspace then gets the
label and appears in the label’s study pad. A bookmark made with an auto-assigned label does not
open the *Bookmark labels* sheet. The [Search](search.md) feature can help
you find all occurrences of key words across your installed Bible modules.

## Word Study

Install a Strong’s dictionary and press the *Strong’s numbers* button in the toolbar until the
words are underlined or numbered. Tap a word: the definition opens, or a menu appears where you
choose *Definition & Morphology*. In the definition, *Find all occurrences* lists every verse that uses
the same Strong’s number, which is what turns a lookup into a word study. See
[Dictionaries](documents.md#dictionaries). Use the
[links window](documents.md) to keep the dictionary visible alongside your Bible text.

## Personal Devotions

Set up a [reading plan](reading_plans.md) and use the [Speak](speak.md) feature to listen
to the text being read aloud. Add personal [Notes](notes.md) to verses that stand out during
your reading. To keep your devotional notes together, create a label such as “Devotions” and make
it auto-assigned (see [Labels](labels.md#change-label-settings)): all your notes and bookmarks then
collect in that label’s [study pad](study_pads.md). A verse you want to learn can be sent to
[Memorize](memorize.md) from the verse action dialog.

## Sermon notes

Use a [study pad](study_pads.md) to organize your sermon outline. You can add verses
from different books and write text entries (the plus button in the study pad) between them.
[Labels](labels.md) can help you categorize your sermon material by topic or series. Make the
sermon’s label auto-assigned (see [Label settings](labels.md#change-label-settings)) while you
prepare, and every bookmark you make in the workspace goes into the sermon’s study pad; switch it
off again when you are done. To share the outline, choose `Export as HTML` or `Export Study Pad`
in the study pad’s window menu (see [Sharing](share.md#sharing-a-study-pad)).

## Original Language Study

Install Strong’s Greek and Hebrew dictionaries and a morphology dictionary (like Robinson’s).
Choose them in *Application preferences*, in the *Dictionaries* section (*Strongs Greek dictionary*,
*Strongs Hebrew dictionary* and *Robinson Greek morphology*). Press the *Strong’s numbers* button in the toolbar
to show the original language codes. Tap words and choose *Definition & Morphology* to see definitions and parsing information.
Use multiple [Windows](windows.md) to compare translations side by side.
