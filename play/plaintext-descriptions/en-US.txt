Powerful Bible Study tool

"AndBible: Bible Study" is a powerful, easy-to-use, offline Bible study app for Android — a versatile toolkit of high-quality features serving many study styles, from casual reading to in-depth research. More than a reader, it's a tool for serious personal study.

Developed by Bible readers, for Bible readers — a non-profit, open-source community project that is completely free and contains no ads.

Many popular Bible versions are available including KJV, NASB, NET, and others. Popular commentaries like Matthew Henry and John Gill are also available.

Powerful Bible Study features

The application has many insightful and original features that makes a complex and deep Bible study experience smoother than ever. The most notable features are:

 * Split text views that enable comparing translations and consulting commentaries
 * Workspaces allow multiple Bible study setups with their own settings
 * Strong's integration allows Greek and Hebrew word analysis
 * Tap any link to follow hyperlinked cross-references, footnotes, and commentaries (Gill, Matthew Henry etc.) or cross-reference collections (Treasure of Scripture Knowledge, TSKe).
 * Optional AI study agent (opt-in only — requires your own API key for an external service): explore your installed commentaries and dictionaries with AI assistance in your chosen language, plus other creative study workflows. A built-in permission system keeps you in full control of what the agent can do.
 * Flexible full-text search across one or many Bible translations simultaneously
 * Advanced bookmarking & highlighting features with personal study notes
 * Study Pads for adding notes and Bible references while listening to sermons
 * Reading and memorization tracking: set reading goals, track progress, and memorize verses with interactive Word Scramble, Word Order, Word Blur, and Type It modes
 * Advanced text to speech with speak bookmarks, enabling a smooth Bible listening experience
 * Cross-device sync: keep your workspaces, bookmarks, notes, and reading progress in sync across all your devices
 * Vast library of documents: Bible translations, theological commentaries, dictionaries, maps, and christian books, totaling over 1500 documents in over 700 languages, legally distributed by Crosswire and other SWORD repositories
 * Native support for MyBible, MySword and EPUB files enables you to extend your library even more

Let’s make the best Bible app together!

AndBible is an open-source community project. In practice, this means that anyone with suitable skills can, and is encouraged to, contribute to the project by:

 * developing new features,
 * testing not-yet-released features,
 * keeping user interface translations up-to-date, and
 * helping to extend the module library by obtaining permissions from copyright holders, or by converting documents to SWORD format.

If you are a professional software engineer or tester, please consider contributing to the project. For more information on how to contribute, please see https://git.io/JUnaj.
